This page describes the current Luma app and this download website. Last updated October 24, 2026.

On your Mac

When you select Remember login, Luma stores your provider credentials in macOS Keychain. If you leave it off, your login is used for the current session without being saved to Keychain. Watch history, Watchlist, favorites and playback preferences are stored locally on your Mac and are not synced to a Luma account or cloud service.

Signing out removes the saved provider login. Your locally stored viewing history and lists remain associated with that provider account so they can be restored when you sign in again.

Connections to your provider

Luma sends your login to the provider server you enter to retrieve your library and play streams. It also loads artwork and streaming resources from addresses supplied by that provider. Your provider and those hosting services can receive your IP address and requests. Their own privacy policies apply. Choose a provider you trust and use its HTTPS server address when available.

No Luma analytics

The app does not include Luma advertising, tracking analytics or a Luma-operated account service. It uses the bundled VideoLAN playback engine to play your content.

This website and downloads

This static website does not add analytics, tracking cookies, external fonts or advertising. GitHub hosts the website and app downloads and may process technical information such as IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Support

Support is available through GitHub issues. These reports are public. Do not include passwords, provider login URLs or screenshots containing account details.
