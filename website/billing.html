LUMA FOR MAC · VERSION 0.8.0

Three days to
make yourself at home.

Try the full Mac app for 72 hours, without a payment card. The trial starts when you choose Start my 3-day free trial in Luma. Nothing is charged automatically when it ends.

€4 per month, if you choose to subscribe

After the trial ends, choose Subscribe · €4/month in Luma Access to open Stripe Checkout. Your first payment is due when you subscribe. The subscription then renews at €4 each month until canceled. Stripe shows the amount and recurring payment terms before you confirm payment. Payment details go directly to Stripe.

A subscription unlocks Luma for Mac on up to two Macs. It does not include TV channels, movies, series, or a provider subscription. Bring your own authorized Xtream Codes account or M3U playlist. Windows 0.7.3 remains a free preview and does not use this subscription.

Cancel from Luma

Choose Luma → Luma Access… → Manage or cancel subscription. The Stripe customer portal lets you cancel, update your payment method and view invoices. Cancel before the next renewal to prevent that renewal charge. Access continues until the end of the paid period. Cancellation does not itself refund a previous payment; contact billing support if you need help with a charge.

Keep your recovery key

In Luma Access, use Copy my product key and store the key privately. It restores your existing access after reinstalling or moving to another Mac. Do not share it publicly. If you lose your key or need to release an old Mac from your two-device limit, contact support with your purchase email or invoice reference. We may need to verify ownership.

Internet access and your library

Luma verifies access online at launch and periodically while running. A brief connection outage can use the remaining verification period, up to five minutes; playback stops if access cannot be verified after that. Your provider credentials, playlists and viewing history stay on your computer and are not uploaded to Luma’s licensing service. Read the privacy information.

Updating from a free 0.7.x Mac release to 0.8.0 introduces the trial and subscription. Your saved library data stays on your Mac. Older free builds are not retroactively locked.
