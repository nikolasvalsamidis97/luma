This page describes the current Luma app and this download website. Last updated October 26, 2026.

On your computer

When you select Remember login, Luma stores playlist names, provider credentials or M3U URLs, and the selected playlist in macOS Keychain on Mac, or encrypted with Windows DPAPI for your Windows login. If you leave it off, your login is used for the current session without saving that connection. Other saved playlists remain available. Watch history, Watchlist, favorites and playback preferences are stored locally on your computer and are not synced to a Luma account or cloud service.

Disconnect keeps saved playlists. Removing a playlist through Manage playlists deletes its saved login. Your locally stored viewing history and lists remain associated with that provider account so they can be restored when you sign in again.

Connections to your provider

Luma sends your login to the Xtream Codes server you enter, or downloads the M3U URL you provide, to retrieve your library and play streams. Playlist URLs can include access credentials. It also loads artwork and streaming resources from addresses supplied by that provider. Your provider and those hosting services can receive your IP address and requests. Their own privacy policies apply. Choose a provider you trust and use its HTTPS server address when available.

Mac activation and subscriptions

Mac version 0.8.0 connects to Luma’s licensing service, hosted by Cloudflare Workers and D1. When you start a trial or activate a product key, the app sends a hashed Mac hardware identifier and a random activation token over HTTPS. The service stores the hashed device identifier, hashes of activation tokens and product keys, trial and access dates, device activations, Stripe customer/subscription/checkout identifiers, and identifiers of processed payment events. The activation token and recovery product key are saved in macOS Keychain.

The service uses these records to provide the 72-hour trial, verify subscriptions and developer keys, enforce device limits, restore access and prevent repeated trials. Verification occurs at launch and periodically while the app is running. Your provider credentials, playlist contents, viewing history and streamed media are not sent to this service. IP addresses are used for request rate limiting; temporary rate-limit records use hashed identifiers and expired counters are removed by an hourly cleanup. Cloudflare may process network and security information under its privacy policy.

Stripe hosts checkout and the customer billing portal. If you subscribe, Stripe receives the payment and billing information you enter, including your email address. Luma’s operator can view billing records in Stripe to manage payments and support requests. Card numbers and card security codes are not handled by Luma’s app or licensing server. See Stripe’s Privacy Policy.

Licensing records are retained until removed by the operator; uninstalling the app does not delete server-side trial or purchase records. To request access, correction or deletion of your information, contact nikolasvalsamidis97@gmail.com. We may need to verify ownership. Deleting an activation record does not cancel a Stripe subscription: use the billing portal to cancel first, or ask support for help. Stripe’s retention of payment records is governed by its own policies and applicable requirements.

No Luma analytics

Luma does not include advertising or tracking analytics. It uses the bundled VideoLAN playback engine. The Windows preview and older free Mac 0.7.x builds do not connect to Luma’s licensing service; their library data remains local.

This website and downloads

This static website does not add analytics, tracking cookies, external fonts or advertising. GitHub hosts the website and app downloads and may process technical information such as IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
