GOOD TO KNOW

A few small details.

Does Luma include channels or a subscription?

No. Luma plays content from your own provider. You’ll need a working Xtream Codes account or M3U playlist link and permission to access the content you watch.

Do I need to install anything else?

No extra player or playback tools are needed. Luma includes its playback engine. You just need a supported computer, an internet connection and your provider account.

Can it play 4K and multiple languages?

Luma supports 4K playback and lets you select available audio and subtitle tracks. Availability, picture quality and smooth playback depend on your provider’s stream, your connection and your computer. A quality selector appears when the stream offers selectable variants.

Where are my login and watch history stored?

Saved playlist logins stay in macOS Keychain or are encrypted for your Windows login with DPAPI. Watch history, Watchlist and preferences stay on your computer, with separate libraries for each provider. Luma connects directly to your provider to retrieve and play your content. Read the privacy information for more details.

Can I save more than one playlist?

Yes. Click Add playlist in the sidebar and leave Remember login enabled. Use the playlist menu to switch, and Manage playlists to rename or remove saved connections. Disconnect keeps your saved playlists for next time.

How do I update Luma or report a problem?

Download the latest release here and replace the app in Applications on Mac, or extract the new Windows ZIP into a new folder. To report a problem, open a GitHub issue. Please leave passwords and provider login links out of public reports.
